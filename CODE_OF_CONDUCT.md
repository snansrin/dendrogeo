# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussion and review.
- Explain scientific, technical, and accessibility concerns with evidence and care.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and work toward a repair.
- Respect privacy, consent, authorship, and data licensing decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publication or misuse of private data without permission are not acceptable. Sustained disruption of discussion or review is also not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project events. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report concerns privately to sinan@dendrogeo.org. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. The maintainers will review the report, hear relevant parties where appropriate, and communicate the decision and any action. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is not acceptable.
